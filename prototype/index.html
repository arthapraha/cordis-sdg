Methodological Findings & Constraints

  • Vocabulary Divergence & The Recall Ceiling (0.442): Keyword and text-matching pipelines cannot reach 53 of 95 gold-standard target associations because CORDIS project descriptions and UN SDG target texts use non-overlapping terminologies. This ceiling is an inherent property of lexical overlap, not a pipeline bug.
  • Unassigned Rate (30.6%): 7,175 of 23,451 projects receive no SDG assignment (assignment_level: none). Calibrated thresholding intentionally rejects low-confidence matches to preserve high precision (0.447) rather than forcing arbitrary labels.
  • Single-Reference Annotation Limit: The reference labels for the 100 evaluation projects come from a single annotator. A planned second annotation of 20 projects by the author was not made, so no adjudication took place and this limitation covers all 100.
  • Deterministic Verification & Explanation Pass: To ensure 100% deterministic reproducibility without external API key dependencies, model-generated explanations were parked under the methodology specification during the frozen run.

Methodology & Provenance

Deterministic classification methodology, taxonomy integration, reproducibility commands, and cryptographic verification digests for the Horizon Europe CORDIS SDG mapping analysis.

What the data is

The analysis operates on official European Union open datasets and established international taxonomies:

  • The Horizon Europe projects: A complete snapshot of 23,451 research projects funded under the European Union's Horizon Europe framework programme (2021–2027), sourced from the official CORDIS open data repository. Each project record contains project identifiers, title, acronym, teaser summary, comprehensive objective text, free keywords, and classified scientific domains.
  • UN Sustainable Development Goals Taxonomy: The 17 Goals and 169 Targets defined by the United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, sourced from the Publications Office of the European Union (EU Vocabularies). The taxonomy establishes standard descriptions, indicators, and official target statements.
  • European Science Vocabulary (EuroSciVoc): The multilingual taxonomy of scientific fields maintained by the Publications Office of the European Union. A curated, peer-reviewed domain crosswalk containing 235 mappings connects EuroSciVoc disciplines directly to relevant SDG targets and goals.
  • Five Analyzed Text Fields: Project evidence is extracted across five distinct fields: Project Title, Teaser, Objective, Free Keywords, and EuroSciVoc Fields. The pipeline explicitly tracks which fields are populated in each CORDIS record to distinguish between absent text fields and non-matching text.

What the pipeline does

The system performs deterministic multi-label classification to assign UN SDGs to Horizon Europe research projects:

  • Dual Evidence Architecture: The classifier synthesises two complementary channels of evidence:
    • Lexical Target Matching: High-precision phrase and keyword matching using strict word-boundary regular expressions against SDG target definitions, guarded by negative filters and domain stop-lists to eliminate polysemous false positives.
    • Taxonomic Crosswalk: Domain-level assignments inferred from official EuroSciVoc classifications attached to each project.
  • Assignment Tiers & Calibrated Precision: Projects are stratified into three mutually distinct assignment tiers:
    • Target-Level Assigned (40.1%, 9,394 projects): Substantive evidence meeting calibrated scoring thresholds for one or more specific SDG targets.
    • Goal-Only Assigned (29.3%, 6,882 projects): High-level relevance to broader SDG themes without meeting granular target-level evidence criteria.
    • Unassigned (30.6%, 7,175 projects): Projects lacking sufficient lexical or taxonomic overlap are left unassigned, preserving high precision (0.447) rather than introducing speculative labels.
  • Full Attribution & Auditability: Every assigned target or goal retains complete evidence provenance, recording the exact matched phrases and the specific project fields in which they occurred.
  • Source Text Fidelity: All project titles and acronyms are preserved verbatim from raw CORDIS source bytes without normalization or escaping (including leading hyphens in projects 101073045 and 101275778).

What was frozen and when

Methodological specifications, logic, and evaluation artifacts were formally frozen on 12 September 2026:

  • Pipeline Freeze (12 September 2026): The classification algorithm, matching rules, scoring weights, vocabulary stop-lists, and assignment thresholds were frozen at commit 0246412b44f722567feaa2e6c1b6e695de86c923.
  • Single Blind Evaluation Run: Evaluation was performed once against the frozen pipeline using a held-out benchmark of 100 projects. The run yielded target-level precision of 0.447 and recall of 0.221, reflecting an inherent vocabulary ceiling of 0.442 where 53 of 95 reference pairs share zero lexical overlap with official UN target texts.
  • Fast run checked against the slow run: A performance optimization (substring pre-check skip) was verified against a slow control run across all 23,451 projects (28,833 assigned rows). Two independent instruments confirmed byte-identical results: zero missing projects, zero row differences, and zero score or band discrepancies.
  • Frozen Run Output: The full evaluation output hashes cryptographically to 0938fe34e41cd5d8ddccfba69238481fdf6470e54046104ad7a3535da6743709.
